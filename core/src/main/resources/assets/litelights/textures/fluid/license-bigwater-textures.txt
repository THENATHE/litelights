Large water and lava textures

water_still.png, water_flow.png, lava_still.png and lava_flow.png (and their .png.mcmeta animation
files) in this folder are the vanilla-style textures of Big Water by Tec, from
https://github.com/Tectato/BigWater (src/main/resources/resourcepacks/vanilla/assets/bigwater/textures/block/).

The PNG files were losslessly recompressed to make the jar smaller: the lava textures saved as RGB
(every pixel was fully opaque) and the water textures as an exact six-colour palette with their
alpha kept. Every decoded pixel is identical to the original, checked with Minecraft's own image
decoder.

lava_flow.png was also cropped: LiteLights shows only every second of its 128 frames (the strip
it builds holds at most 8192 pixels per column), so only those 64 frames are kept, each pixel
identical to the original's, and lava_flow.png.mcmeta's frametime is doubled from 2 to 4 so the
animation runs at the same speed. The other .png.mcmeta files are unchanged.

Big Water: https://modrinth.com/mod/big-water

They are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

LiteLights uses only these texture files. The idea of large water textures is credited to Tec
(Big Water); LiteLights' code and shaders for them are its own and were written without reading
Big Water's code.
