# Credits

LiteLights is written from scratch. Where an idea came from another project, it is credited here as "idea by". No code, shaders or data were copied from those projects, with one exception noted below (Big Water's textures, which are CC0).

- **Rendering ideas** (cached shadow map, forward-shaded effects, biome atmosphere): idea by Shinyflvres (ImmersiveVFX), whose atmosphere effects were in turn inspired by Oreville Studios' Realistic Biomes.
- **Large water textures:** idea by Tec (Big Water). Vanilla-style water and lava textures by Tec, from Big Water, CC0-1.0.
- **Jump splash:** idea by Chai (Particular).
- **Storm atmosphere** (sandstorm fog, blizzards, swamp and pale garden fog, ambient wind, dark pale-garden rain): ideas by TheDeathlyCow (Immersive Storms).
- **Lightning in the clouds** (flashes flickering inside the clouds, lighting them and the land): idea by comedy (Squall).
- **Branching lightning** (jagged, forking bolts instead of straight ones): idea by Moth_SKW (Tempestra).
- **Weather on glass** (frost, stuck snow, dust after sandstorms): glass weather idea by duffopuffo (Weather Glass).
- **Rain drips and wall streaks** (on walls and glass): drip and streak ideas by pigcart (Particle Rain).
- **Wakes** (behind boats and swimmers, rings where things fall in, bow spray): wake idea by Goby56 (Wakes). Written from scratch from Wakes' public description and the textbook wave equation; no code, shaders or textures used.
- **Falling leaves** (from every kind of tree, drifting on the wind): idea by Fourmisain (Falling Leaves). Written from its public description only; LiteLights' falling leaves are vanilla's own leaf particles, steered by LiteLights' wind.
- **Rainbows** (after rain and thunderstorms): rainbow idea by AppleNButter (You Must Have The Rain). Written from scratch from its public description; no code, shaders or textures used.
- **Snowy leaves and ground** (leaves whitening while it snows, a dusting under trees): idea by IMB11 (Snow, formerly Snow Under Trees). Written from scratch from its public description; no code, shaders or textures used.
- **Swaying lanterns** (hanging lanterns, their chains and hanging signs swinging in the wind): idea by Rilith (Swinging Lanterns). Written from scratch from its public description; no code used.
- **Heat shimmer** (the air wavering over lava, fire and hot ground): idea by stepqq (Lava Particles | More), from its heat waves over lava geysers.
- **Snow on blocks** (visual snow: snow on stairs, slabs, walls and fences, and plants and fences standing in snow): idea by LambdAurora (LambdaBetterGrass), whose better snow draws snow on blocks that can't hold it. Written from scratch from what it draws in game; no code, models, textures or block lists used (LiteLights finds the blocks by the game's own classes and shapes).
- **Snow over edges** (visual snow: snow weeping down the sides of blocks under any snow's edge): idea by Snownee (Snow! Real Magic). Written from scratch from its public description; no code, shaders, models or textures used (the drips are LiteLights' own geometry with the game's snow texture).
- **Dynamic clouds** (big voxel clouds of real height, in formations that grow and shrink, with wider cubes far away): idea by nonamecrackers2 (Simple Clouds), the mod the cloud idea came from. Simple Clouds was studied for the idea. LiteLights' clouds and their cloud field are its own; the layout of cube sizes by distance (8-block cubes that double in rings, the near ring's radius and the cloud shadow map's size) has the same values as Simple Clouds', which LiteLights then checked against its own measurements. No code, shaders, data or wording were copied.
- **The sky's clouds and weather in step** (the drawn clouds, and the rain and thunder shown under them, worked out from one shared description of the sky that every player's game works out alike): idea by ProtoManly (ProtoManly's Weather). No code, shaders, data or wording used.
- **No rain over the clouds' tops, and cloud shadows drifting over the land:** ideas by LockBoss (Create: Sky). No code, shaders, data or wording used.
- **Far terrain** (the land drawn far past the render distance): follows the idea of far terrain in Voxy by MCRcortex and Distant Horizons by the Distant Horizons team. LiteLights' far terrain is written from scratch; no code, shaders, data or wording of either was used.
- **Seed generation** (far land not visited yet, predicted from the world's seed): idea by Möbius (Voxy SeedGen). Written from the game's own classes and the author's own earlier seed-generation projects (`voxy-seedgen-companion`, whose lessons are the author's own work); nothing of Voxy SeedGen's code, data or wording, or of its port, was used.
- **Overlays module:** draws through Continuity (Pepper_Bell).
- **Recommended:** LambDynamicLights and LambdaBetterGrass by LambdAurora, Visuality by PinkGoosik, and Subtle Effects by MincraftEinstein.

- **Big Water's vanilla-style water and lava textures** (`assets/litelights/textures/fluid/`): CC0 1.0 Universal, by Tec. What was changed (lossless recompression, and only the shown frames of `lava_flow.png` kept) is in `assets/litelights/textures/fluid/license-bigwater-textures.txt`, which ships in the jar beside them.
- **Squall** and **Tempestra** are not included, and none of their code, shaders or textures were opened: lightning in the clouds and the branching bolts were written from their public descriptions only.
- **Weather Glass** (PolyForm Shield) and **Particle Rain** are not included, and none of their code, shaders or textures were opened: weather on glass, drips and wall streaks were written from their public descriptions only.
- **Wakes** (Goby56's, and its port Wakes Reforged; both GPL-3.0) is not included, and none of its code, shaders, textures or design notes were opened: LiteLights' wakes were written from its public description, changelog and pictures only.
- **"Hash without Sine"** by Dave Hoskins (MIT licence, Copyright (c)2014 David Hoskins, https://www.shadertoy.com/view/4djSRW): the three small hash functions in `shaders/include/noise.glsl` are his widely shared snippet. His copyright and permission notice is above them there, and in `LICENSE`.
- **Minecraft's core shaders:** the files in `shaders/core/` are modified copies of Mojang's vanilla shaders. They are still Mojang's and aren't covered by LiteLights' MIT licence; only LiteLights' own changes are. `shaders/core/readme.txt` says the same.
- **Soft rain splash sprites** (`textures/particle/rain_splash_soft_0` to `_3`): the same shapes as vanilla's rain splashes, recoloured white. They are a few pixels each, and we treat them as free use.
- **Mod icon:** an oak tree made from vanilla's oak log and leaf textures (`tools/render_icon.py`). Our position is that it's a transparent, actively artistic free use of them.
- **Sodium** (PolyForm Shield 1.0.0) is not included and none of its code is copied. When Sodium is installed, LiteLights' Sodium terrain shader includes Sodium's own shader includes from Sodium's jar at run time, and LiteLights' pages in Sodium's Video Settings use Sodium's public config API.
- **Continuity** is not included; the overlays module calls its public API when it is installed.
